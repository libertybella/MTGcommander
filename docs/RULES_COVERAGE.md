# Rules Coverage (Engine V1 through Checkpoint 36)

This document records what the engine implements and what it intentionally does not. It is not a complete CR translation.

## Implemented

- 2–4 player Commander game state, turns, priority, and a last-in first-out stack.
- Zone movement with owner-zone integrity, including a `removed` zone for players who have left the game.
- Casting spells from hand, playing one land per turn, combat declarations, concede.
- Choose-on-cast targeting; legality at cast and again on resolve. If no legal targets remain, the spell’s effects are skipped (fizzle) and the card still goes to its destination. Targets may be players, creatures, or spells on the stack.
- Hidden information projection: opponent hands and libraries hide card identity; battlefield, graveyard, exile, command, stack, life, and commander damage stay public. The UI shows `GameHost.viewFor`, not raw authority.
- Basic effects: damage, life, draw, mill (mill what’s there), discard (front of hand), sacrifice, counters, tokens, tap/untap, mana, zone moves.
- `tap_for_mana` for permanents whose definition has `manaAbilities`, or legacy `produces` / `producesOptions` / `producesAnyColor`. Multiple `{T}: Add` abilities are distinct choices (`manaIndex`). Pain lands deal that damage after the tap. Mana abilities do not use the stack. Dual lands and Command Tower ask for a color.
- Non-mana activated abilities: `activate_ability` pays `{T}` and/or a simple mana cost, then puts an ability on the stack. Creatures with a tap cost respect summoning sickness. Targets are chosen on activate and rechecked on resolve. Channel (`discard` from hand) pays the cost and resolves immediately, with no round of priority.
- A 22-card synthetic pool and catalog seating (`startCatalogGame`). Still available from the start screen.
- Real cards: Scryfall-shaped `OracleCard` data compiles into `CardDefinition` by matching known oracle sentences. Instant/sorcery patterns include damage, life, draw, mill, surveil, destroy/exile/bounce, counter target spell / creature spell / noncreature spell, destroy target nonartifact creature, ritual mana, simple tokens, `amass [subtype] N` (N defaults to 1), `discard a card unless you attacked this turn`, targeted `reveals their hand` plus choose/exile/discard (including Duress-style noncreature, nonland), `target player draws N and loses N life`, `Scry N, then draw M`, and look-at-top-N assign to hand/bottom/exile. Creatures get printed P/T, known keywords, `When ~ enters` triggers (including targeted clauses the engine already knows, combined ETB+attack sentences that compile the ETB half, and legendary short-name `When Gothmog enters` → `When ~ enters`), `At the beginning of combat on your turn` triggers, and `Creatures you control get +N/+N`. Lands/artifacts tap via `{T}: Add {M}`, `{T}: Add {G} or {U}`, or `{T}: Add one mana of any color`. Multiple `{T}: Add` lines compile as separate mana abilities, including pain-land damage. `{N}, {T}:` abilities using those effects compile. `Channel — {cost}, Discard this card:` compiles as a hand activated ability when the effect clause is known. `{cost}: Level N` on Class enchantments compiles as a sorcery-speed upgrade. Modal DFCs keep both faces (`otherFaceId`) so either face can be played; seating copies the back face into `game.definitions`. `~ enters tapped` and `If you control two or more other lands, ~ enters tapped` compile as replacements, including `unless you control two or more basic lands`. Leftover sentences are notes.
- Deck import: Moxfield public URL (Electron IPC) or pasted Commander/Arena text. `startDefinitionGame` shuffles and seats 2, 3, or 4 players. Empty opponent URLs mirror your deck. Compile notes are shown; uncompiled cards stay in the deck.
- Local React battlefield for 2, 3, or 4 players. Actions go through `GameHost.submit`. Optional local hotseat seats every player at this PC; otherwise unseated opponents auto-pass. Authority persists in localStorage for local tables. Oracle cache key `mtgcommander.oracle.v3`. Mouseover of a double-faced card shows both faces with the current face highlighted, clamped inside the window. Playing a modal DFC asks which face. Class permanents show a level badge and highlight the current class text. Channel from hand is offered next to playing the card as a land and resolves without a pass-around.
- London mulligan before the first turn: keep or shuffle-and-draw-7, then put counted cards on the bottom. In 3–4 player games the first mulligan is free (CR 103.5c).
- Optional WebSocket table: Electron hosts on port 8787 with a room code. Friends join with a display name and receive `viewFor` themselves. Unjoined seats still auto-pass.
- Keywords: flying, reach, haste, vigilance, trample, deathtouch, lifelink, first strike, double strike, menace, hexproof, indestructible, flash, defender.
- Derived power/toughness: printed values, `p1p1` counters, and simple static P/T modifiers (`self` or `controlled_creatures`).
- Enter-the-battlefield and beginning-of-combat triggers become stack abilities. Targeted ETBs pause on `GameState.prompts` until `choose_targets`. If no legal target exists, the trigger is skipped (CR 603.3d). Unseated players auto-pick the first legal target. Simultaneous trigger order is still not chosen. Beginning of combat is still skipped when the stack and prompts are empty.
- Draw replacement: a controlled `replace_draw` / `skip` permanent skips draws. Enter-tapped replacements: unconditional `~ enters tapped`; `unless you control two or more other lands`; `unless you control two or more basic lands`; `unless you control a legendary creature`; `unless you control a Swamp or a Mountain` (any listed type); `If you control two or more other lands, ~ enters tapped` (Hive-style, the inverse of a slow land). Shock lands pause to pay life or enter tapped. Scry and surveil are keyword actions: the owner privately sees the looked-at library cards and chooses keep vs bottom (scry) or keep vs graveyard (surveil). Other players do not get priority during that choice. A scry/surveil-only ETB (Temple of Epiphany) opens the prompt immediately instead of stacking a trigger. Spells like Consider still use the stack; after they resolve, the look has no extra pass-around. Unseated seats auto-keep. `amass` creates a 0/0 Army token if you control none, then puts that many +1/+1 counters on an Army you control. Chart a Course-style spells discard unless `attackedThisTurn`. Targeted hand reveal is private to the caster (`GameState.reveals`), then a reusable choose-card step handles the after-effect (exile, discard). Expressive Iteration-style look-and-assign puts one card in hand, one on the bottom, and one in exile; “you may play the exiled card this turn” stays a note. Class enchantments enter at level 1; `{cost}: Level N` upgrades one level as a sorcery. Class keyword abilities other than the level-up cost stay notes.
- State-based actions: 0 life, 21 commander damage, 0 toughness (0 toughness dies even if indestructible), and failing to draw from an empty library. Lethal damage destruction still runs after combat damage.
- Stack targeting: spells on the stack can be chosen (`kind: "spell"`). Test Counter counters a targeted spell; the countered card goes to the graveyard (commanders still return to the command zone).
- Public game log: zone changes, life changes, and table overrides. The UI shows the viewer’s projection.
- Manual override: a seated living player may adjust life, draw, mill, add one mana, tap/untap, or move a public card without priority. In Override mode, clicking one of your battlefield cards toggles tap/untap. Right-click a card you control that can produce a token to create that token at will. In hotseat, player options and Override apply to the player you are currently acting as, not the South camera seat. Hidden opponent hand/library cards cannot be moved. Blocked during mulligan and after game over.

## Documented gaps

- Rooms exist as one in-memory table per Electron host. No matchmaking, no accounts, no cloud hosting. Friends need the host IP (LAN or Tailscale) and room code.
- Full oracle-text parsing. Unmatched sentences stay as notes. Hybrid pips `{R/W}` are payable. Phyrexian and `{X}` costs are unpayable. Command Tower–style lands tap for any color; commander identity is not enforced.
- Scryfall bulk JSON is not shipped in git. Cache is filled on demand. A plain browser tab cannot fetch Moxfield (CORS); paste the export instead.
- Face-down cards (morph/manifest). Opponent hand/library identity is hidden; there is no face-down battlefield state.
- Ward, shroud, protection, hexproof on players, and targeting abilities on the stack.
- Full APNAP trigger ordering, modal trigger choices, and leave-the-game triggers. Targeted ETBs now pause for a target; other trigger events still do not.
- Layers, copy effects, timestamps, and dependency. Static P/T is a V1 shortcut, not CR 613.
- Damage replacement, prevention, and multiple replacements interacting.
- Search, modal spell choices, and a general choice system beyond trigger targets.
- Combat damage assignment order is the blocker list order; players cannot reorder.
- Keyword counters, keyword-granting effects, and anthem effects other than the static P/T selectors above.
- Other trigger events, loyalty abilities, until-end-of-turn pumps, modal spells, search, and oracle compile of Phyrexian/`{X}`. Attack-trigger amass (Warg Rider) and “then the Army deals damage” stay notes; right-click create token is the backup. “You may play the exiled card this turn” and Ring tempts / Sauron discard-hand are not compiled. Activated abilities cannot yet be targeted by counterspells. Use table override until those compile.
- Commander ninjutsu, partner details beyond stored commander IDs, and dethrone-style combat restrictions.
